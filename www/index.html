📖 User manual & why Navora

Camera locations: OpenStreetMap + community reports. Traffic: TomTom. Weather: MET Malaysia.

🆘 Emergency

📞 999 — Police / Ambulance / Fire 📞 PLUSLine 1-800-88-0000 — PLUS highway help 📞 LLM 1-800-88-7752 — Highway Authority

🔒 Privacy

  • No account, no sign-in, no phone number.
  • Your GPS position stays on your phone. It is sent only to TomTom to calculate routes and search — never stored by Navora.
  • Reports are anonymous (a random device ID, no personal data). Your location is shared only when you tap a report button.
  • No advertising trackers, no analytics SDKs, no data sold to anyone.
  • Built in Malaysia. Data sources: TomTom (traffic, maps), MET Malaysia (weather), OpenStreetMap (cameras), public highway and police feeds.
